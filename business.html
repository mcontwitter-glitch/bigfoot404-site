About Bigfoot404 LLC

Bigfoot404 LLC is a New York-based real estate wholesaling company. We identify owner-listed properties, make fast cash offers, and connect motivated sellers with a nationwide network of cash buyers — turning slow, uncertain sales into simple, as-is closings. Alongside the real estate business, we built and operate the BIG Network: our own company blockchain, wallet, and explorer, run with the same standards we hold our real estate operations to.
