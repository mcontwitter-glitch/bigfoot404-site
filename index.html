The Next Evolution of MPL-404 and RWA Utility

Our vision is clear: utilize swap fees from our token supply to fund real-world real estate developments, documenting every step of the process on video — from acquiring the property to building it from the ground up. MPL-404 is a new standard that ties digital assets to real-world property developments. Through a DAO proposal, Bigfoot 404 aims to create a bridge between NFTs and real-world assets, allowing NFT holders access to physical property developments.

  • Partnership programs — offering IRL use of the RWA location for businesses, events, and partnerships

FAQ

What is Bigfoot 404?

Bigfoot 404 ties digital assets to real-world real estate. Swap fees from the token supply fund physical property developments, with NFT holders gaining access to those assets and real-world opportunities.

What is MPL-404?

MPL-404 is a new standard that ties digital assets to real-world property developments. Through a DAO proposal, Bigfoot 404 creates a bridge between NFTs and real-world assets, allowing NFT holders access to physical property developments.

What is RWA (Real-World Assets)?

RWA stands for Real-World Assets — in the context of Bigfoot 404, physical real estate developments funded through token swaps and NFT sales. The goal is to build out property developments and offer NFT holders access to these assets and various real-world opportunities.

Can I get involved in the real-world development process?

Yes! As a Bigfoot 404 NFT holder, you'll have the opportunity to get involved through IRL job opportunities, community contributions, and potential partnerships.

How can I participate in Bigfoot 404?

Acquire Bigfoot 404 tokens, swap them for NFTs, and become part of the community. Stay tuned for the official NFT mint and property development announcements, as well as opportunities to join the DAO.
